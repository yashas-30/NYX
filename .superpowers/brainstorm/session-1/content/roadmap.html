Selected Sub-project Comparison

Why Sub-project 1 first?

  • Establishes database structures to store model user settings & configs.
  • Provides a clean folder taxonomy before we start downloading massive multi-part models or companions.
  • Prevents file name collisions in the models folder.

Impact of delaying Sub-project 1

  • Downloader and Explorer additions will continue writing flat files, increasing folder clutter.
  • Custom model settings (NGL, context) will remain volatile and wipe on app restart.
